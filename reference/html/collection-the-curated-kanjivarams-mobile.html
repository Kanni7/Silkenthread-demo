Collection: Kanjeevaram

 The Kanjeevaram saree, originating from the town of Kanchipuram in Tamil Nadu, India, is renowned for its exquisite craftsmanship and luxurious silk fabric. Known for intricate designs, and use of real gold zari for an unparalleled luxe look. Each Kanjeevaram silk saree is a true heirloom, the craft passed down through generations of weavers, ensuring you get a piece of India's cultural heritage. Silken Thread brings the opulence of pure Kanjeevaram sarees directly to you. Explore our online collection and discover the beauty and the grace of these weaves.
